Frequently asked questions

Are the headphones compatible with my phone?

Yes. Aurora headphones use Bluetooth 5.3 and work with any recent Android phone, iPhone, tablet or laptop. A 3.5 mm cable is included for wired listening.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 2 business days. Delivery usually takes 3 to 5 days in the same region and 7 to 12 days internationally.

Can I return my headphones?

Yes. You have 60 days to return them in their original condition for a full refund, no questions asked.

What does the warranty cover?

The 3-year warranty covers manufacturing defects, battery failure and driver issues. It does not cover accidental damage.
